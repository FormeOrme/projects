## Soups

### Bloody Mary Soup

**Ingredients:**

- 3 tomatoes
- 1 red pepper
- 1/2 lemon
- 1 stalk of celery
- 1 apple
- 1 pinch of salt

**Preparation:** Wash all fruit and vegetables carefully. Juice all ingredients. Serve with ice.

### Carrot Soup

**Ingredients:**

- 5 carrots
- 3 tomatoes
- 1 ginger root
- 1 lemon
- 125 ml water
- 175 g cashew nuts
- 1/2 oz curry
- 1 pinch of salt

**Preparation:** Wash all fruit and vegetables carefully and juice all ingredients. Add the cashew nuts and transfer the mixture to a saucepan. Heat over medium heat.

### Corn Soup

**Ingredients:**

- 1 can of corn
- 1/2 oz onion
- 3 1/2 oz milk

**Preparation:** Drain the corn and put it with the milk into the Juice Art. Add the onion and transfer the mixture into a saucepan. Heat over medium heat.

### Breeze Soup

**Ingredients:**

- 2 tomatoes
- 1 onion
- 1 cucumber
- 1 red pepper
- 1 clove of garlic
- 1 pinch of salt
- 1 pinch of oregano
- 1 pinch of red pepper
- 1 tablespoon of olive oil

**Preparation:** Wash all fruit and vegetables carefully. Juice all ingredients together with the oregano, red pepper, salt and olive oil.

### Spring Soup

**Ingredients:**

- 350 g asparagus
- 1 avocado
- 1 clove of garlic
- 1 pinch of salt
- 1 pinch of cayenne pepper or ground black pepper

**Preparation:** Wash the vegetables carefully. Juice all ingredients, then season with garlic, salt and pepper.

## Frozen Recipes

### Grape Ice Watercress

**Ingredients:**

- 350 g grapes
- 1/2 cup of milk
- 1 tablespoon of honey

**Preparation:** Wash the grapes and put them into the Juice Art with the milk. Add honey to the juice and store in the freezer for 6 hours.

### Watermelon Ice Watercress

**Ingredients:**

- 2 slices of watermelon
- 1/2 cup of milk
- 1 tablespoon of honey

**Preparation:** Remove the peel from the watermelon and put it with the milk into the Juice Art. Add honey to the juice and store in the freezer for 6 hours.

### Kiwi Ice Watercress

**Ingredients:**

- 4 kiwis
- 1/2 cup of milk
- 1 tablespoon of honey

**Preparation:** Remove the peel from the kiwis and add the milk in the Juice Art. Add honey to the juice and store in the freezer for 6 hours.

### Soft Cream

**Ingredients:**

- 4 plums
- 3 tablespoons of raspberries
- 3 tablespoons of blueberries

**Preparation:** Wash all fruit carefully and put it into the Juice Art. For a sweeter taste, add a jar of creamy white yogurt and serve.
